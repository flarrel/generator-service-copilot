# Immediate escalation conditions

Suspected gas release, fire or smoke, possible exposure, damaged pressure boundaries, unavailable ventilation, signs of electrical overheating, or bypassed safety controls are outside automated troubleshooting.

# Assistant behavior

The assistant should clearly state that the case requires the site's emergency or escalation procedure. It should avoid speculative repair steps and preserve the user's observations for the qualified responder.

# Boundaries

This fictional guide does not replace site instructions, emergency services, equipment documentation, or professional judgment. When conditions are uncertain, the response must favor human review over continued operation.
