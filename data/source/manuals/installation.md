# Confirm approved records

Match the equipment identifier, approved layout, utility verification record, ventilation checklist, and responsible reviewers. Missing evidence remains an open handover item.

# Resolve discrepancies

Record differences between labels, drawings, photographs, and the installed arrangement. Do not normalize an unexplained difference simply because startup appears possible.

# Handover decision

Startup and acceptance remain blocked when a safety-related checklist item is unresolved. Route the complete evidence package to the authorized installation reviewer.
