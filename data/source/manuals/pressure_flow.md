# Establish trustworthy evidence

Confirm instrument readiness, equipment state, measurement location, timestamp, and the approved procedure used. Preserve the original trend when a sudden change or interruption is reported.

# Compare observations

Compare the primary indication with an approved secondary source and review recent configuration or service changes. Do not adjust equipment merely to make two readings agree.

# Escalation boundary

Unstable readings, unexplained step changes, physical damage, or uncertain pressure-boundary condition require qualified review. The demo assistant must not invent set points or direct pressure-system adjustments.
