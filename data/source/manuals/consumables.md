# Verify identity

Match the consumable identifier, equipment family, packaging condition, and service record before use. A similar appearance is not evidence that a part is approved.

# Preserve traceability

Record the batch or serial identifier when available, installation date, responsible technician, and the checklist that authorized replacement. Keep removed and installed items distinguishable throughout the service task.

# Quarantine uncertainty

Do not install an item with damaged packaging, unknown storage history, or an identifier that cannot be matched to the approved parts record. Isolate it and request review.
