# Prepare the service record

Identify the equipment, approved checklist version, reason for service, responsible technician, and start condition. Keep observations separate from completed actions.

# Close the work

Record inspected and replaced components, approved methods used, unresolved observations, and required post-service checks. Attach evidence rather than relying on a free-text statement that work is complete.

# Return to service

Only the authorized process can approve return to service. Missing checks, unexplained counter differences, or conditions outside the checklist require review and must remain visible in the record.
