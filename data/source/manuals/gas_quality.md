# Validate the measurement context

Confirm the equipment identifier, sampling point, instrument readiness, timestamp, and applicable approved procedure. A reading without this context must not be treated as a verified product result.

# Compare records

Review the trend, recent service activity, and any independent approved measurement. Preserve disagreements rather than choosing the more convenient value.

# Escalate abnormal results

Unexpected, unstable, or conflicting readings require the approved product-control response and qualified review. The demo assistant must not invent an acceptable range or release product.
